Moving books from Popfeed

Rosetta

Rosetta is how Resenha moves your records from other apps into BookHive records (starting with version 1.6.10).

How it works:

Each Popfeed book becomes a BookHive record when BookHive’s catalogue has it for certain, and a Resenha reading when it doesn’t. Its Popfeed records are then deleted.

Moving book data between apps has a reputation for guessing wrong and losing things. Below is exactly what happens to each book, field by field. Nothing is written until you confirm.

How a book moves

  1. 1

    Backup

    When you confirm, Resenha first saves a file with every record the run will delete or change, exactly as your account has it. It goes to Resenha’s folder in iCloud Drive, which you can open in Files, or stays on the device when iCloud Drive is off. Resenha can’t restore from it yet; it keeps your data.

  2. 2

    Match

    Each book is matched by its numbers, the ISBN or the Goodreads ID. Titles only suggest. You see the whole plan, change what you like, and confirm once. Until then the only records written are your choices, so they follow you to your other devices.

  3. 3

    One commit per book

    A book’s new record and the deletion of its Popfeed records travel in one commit, which your account applies whole or not at all, so a book is never half-moved. Just before, Resenha reads the book’s records again; if anything changed, the book is skipped and offered again at the end. A new record takes its key from the book’s oldest Popfeed record, so a retry finds the first attempt instead of making a copy.

  4. 4

    Paced

    Resenha spends at most 70% of your account’s hourly and daily write budget, by Bluesky’s published limits, and waits before reaching them, so your own posting keeps working. If you close the app, the move picks up where it stopped the next time you open it.

BookHive lists moved books as recent activity. In Resenha they keep their own dates.
