Terms of Use

Resenha is an app for reading and writing book reviews on the Atmosphere, the network of apps built on the AT Protocol. By using Resenha you agree to these terms.

No tolerance for objectionable content or abuse

Resenha shows reviews, posts and profiles written by other people. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don’t use Resenha to publish or share:

  • harassment, threats, or hate directed at people or groups
  • sexual content involving minors, or unwanted sexual content
  • spam, scams, or content meant to mislead
  • anything illegal, or that infringes on someone else’s rights

Reporting and blocking

Every review, post and profile in Resenha has a menu to report it or block its author.

Reports go to the network’s moderators, who can label or remove content and suspend accounts. What you report is hidden for you in Resenha straight away.

Blocking someone hides their reviews, posts and profile in Resenha. Resenha also blocks them in your account, so other apps that respect blocks hide them too.

Resenha’s maintainer reviews reports about content shown in the app and removes content and accounts that break these terms from Resenha within 24 hours.

Your account and your content

Resenha doesn’t create or hold accounts. You sign in with an Atmosphere account from a host such as Bluesky, and its own terms apply too. What you publish is saved to that account and belongs to it. You can remove it at any time.
