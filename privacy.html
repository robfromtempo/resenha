Privacy

Resenha does not have its own accounts, and it does not collect data about you. There is no Resenha server.

On your devices

Signing in keeps a session in the keychain on that device, so you stay logged in. Signing out removes it.

Drafts stay on the device. If iCloud is on, they also sync through your iCloud to your other devices. They are not published, and Resenha does not upload them anywhere else.

When you publish

Publish saves the review to your own Atmosphere account, the same account you use with BookHive. From then on it belongs to that account. Resenha does not keep a copy.

Your password is entered on your account host’s page, such as Bluesky. It is never sent to Resenha.

What we don’t do

No ads. No tracking. No analytics. We don’t sell data, because we don’t hold any.

Feedback

Questions, ideas, and bug reports.
