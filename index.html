Book reviews, in your own words.

A writing app for the books you read, and a reader for them. Write long-form reviews of the books in your BookHive library, read your own eBooks and comics, publish to the open web, and see what the readers you follow are reading.

Available for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Write about the books you love, publish them to the open web, and see what your friends are reading.

Write

A word processor for reviews.

Headings, quotes, lists, code and links, and a title of its own. Add pictures, posts from Bluesky, players and drawings. Mark spoilers, choose the language, and pick up any draft on your other devices through iCloud.

  • Formatting
  • Pictures & posts
  • Kobo highlights
  • Drafts in iCloud

Read

Your own books.

Add EPUB, PDF and CBZ files to your account and they open on all your devices, with your place and your highlights kept in sync through iCloud. Connect a file to a book in your library and, when you finish it, mark it as read or start a review from the last page.

  • EPUB & PDF
  • Highlights
  • Synced through iCloud
  • Furigana

Comics

Comics from the network.

Follow series published on the Atmosphere, read them chapter by chapter, page by page or as a strip, and browse a catalog of every comic on the network by category. Like a chapter, leave a comment, and see the replies in your notifications.

  • Catalog
  • Subscriptions
  • Likes & comments

Follow

The Parlor.

See what the readers you follow are starting, finishing and reviewing, as it happens. Following someone in Resenha is the same follow as on Bluesky and BookHive.

  • Live updates
  • Readers’ shelves
  • Friends’ reviews

Publish

On the open web.

Your reviews live in your own account on the Atmosphere: formatted on resenha.ink, as plain text on BookHive, and posted to Bluesky and Mu Social, where replies become comments.

  • resenha.ink
  • BookHive
  • Bluesky & Mu Social

Import

Bring your books with you.

Coming from Goodreads, StoryGraph or Hardcover? Export your library there and choose the file in Resenha. Every book is found in BookHive’s catalogue, and you check the matches before anything is saved.

  1. Export your library from Goodreads, StoryGraph or Hardcover.
  2. Choose the file in Resenha, which finds each book on BookHive.
  3. Check and import. Ratings, reading status, dates, owned books and reviews come along.

Private notes are never imported.

Collect

Your whole library.

Everything you’ve read, are reading or want to read, in one place. Scan a barcode to add a book, sort your books onto shelves you can share, and see every book on the days you read it in the Timeline.

  • Barcode scanning
  • Shelves
  • Timeline

Remember

See when you read.

The Timeline lays every month out as a calendar, with each book on the days you read it. Scroll back through the years, and fill in the dates of books you read before Resenha.

  • Every month
  • Reading dates
  • Years back
